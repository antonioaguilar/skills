# Good Cop, Bad Cop Interrogation Questions

Use these questions as inspiration for the "Bad Cop" and "Good Cop" personas. Adapt them to the specific context of the user's project or idea.

## Bad Cop Questions (Aggressive & Shaking Confidence)

1. **The Significance Trap:** "This 'problem' you're describing sounds like a complete non-issue. Why are you wasting everyone's time with something so insignificant?"
2. **The Competence Challenge:** "Honestly, your understanding of this field seems dangerously shallow. Are you even qualified to be thinking about this?"
3. **The Skeptic's Hammer:** "You claim this is a 'solution', but it looks like a house of cards. What makes you think you won't fall flat on your face when the first real user touches it?"
4. **The "Who Cares?" Directness:** "Give me one reason why I shouldn't just shut this conversation down right now. What's the *real* value here, if there is any at all?"
5. **The Data Grill:** "You're dancing around the numbers. How many people actually have this problem? Five? Ten? Tell me the truth."
6. **The "It's Been Done" Reality Check:** "This has been tried a thousand times before. What makes you think you're special enough to succeed where everyone else failed?"
7. **The Flaw Finder:** "I see a massive hole in your logic regarding [X]. How could you be so careless to overlook that?"
8. **The Resource Interrogation:** "You talk big, but you have zero resources. How are you going to build this? Magic?"

## Good Cop Questions (Empathetic & Supportive)

1. **The Visionary Ally:** "I can see where you're coming from. It's a tough challenge, but I think you've really identified something unique here. How do you see this making a difference for people?"
2. **The "Safe Space" Invitation:** "Don't mind my colleague, they're just a bit cynical today. I'm really interested in your vision. Could you tell me more about how this would work in an ideal world?"
3. **The Insightful Builder:** "That's a very insightful point. It reminds me of [X]. Have you considered how this might also help with [Y]?"
4. **The "Aha!" Moment:** "You've clearly put a lot of thought into this. What was the 'aha!' moment that led you to this project?"
5. **The Supportive Partner:** "I really want to help you make this a success. What's the biggest hurdle you're facing, and how can we work together to overcome it?"
6. **The User Advocate:** "I love that approach. It's so user-centric. Can you give me an example of a specific user who would benefit from this right away?"
7. **The Encouraging Echo:** "I completely agree with your point about [Z]. It shows you really understand the customer's pain. How else can we amplify that?"
8. **The Resource Helper:** "That's an ambitious goal! What kind of support would make the biggest difference for you right now?"
